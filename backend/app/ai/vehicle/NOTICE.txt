Adapted modules: config, detector, geometry, masks, rules, motion, evidence,
openbed, pose, classifier, evaluate. New live adapter: pipeline.py, __init__.py.
Original rule thresholds and geometry/motion/evidence/open-bed hooks retained.
Offline evaluation/report/source-video reopening and persistent disk feature/
pose caches removed. Evaluation now requires camera-local memory providers.
Open-bed/pose failures propagate, never silently bypass mandatory checks.
Neural models load local safetensors only; initialization/inference serialized.
No archive scripts, training tools, exports or pickle model loaders executed.

Finite-window adaptation is not full-recording equivalence. Records/frames
bounded to at most 60 seconds, 120 records, 256 MiB frame bytes. Minimum 30 seconds
history required before alarms. Startup or insufficient retained history means
warming_up, not a verified negative result. Original 30-minute static-object
probe unavailable in live window. Per-camera affine height calibration and
pixel mask polygons require explicit configuration; original POS masks and
calibrations never auto-applied based only on matching resolution. Classifier
is disabled; its track_people implementation remains needed by evidence.

Output policy: current-frame person voted BED, associated vehicle in_motion,
positive n_bed. Indonesian display label 'Penumpang di bak kendaraan', critical.
Confidence means person detector confidence, not calibrated violation certainty.
